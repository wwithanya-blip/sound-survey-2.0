Speech Perception Study

Purpose

This study explores how people associate the sounds of made-up words with specific sensory qualities (such as weight, sharpness, speed, etc.).

What will I do?

You will listen to pairs of made-up words and answer simple questions like "Which word sounds heavier?" There are no right or wrong answers.

  • Duration: approximately 7 minutes
  • About 43 questions (40 experimental + attention checks)
  • Audio required — please ensure your device can play sound

Risks & Confidentiality

Minimal risk. Your responses are completely anonymous; no personally identifiable information is collected.

Voluntary Participation

Participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time by closing the window.
